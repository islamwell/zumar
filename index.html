Sentence Structure

This verse is a nominal sentence — a subject paired with a predicate, no verb needed

تَنزِيلُ ٱلْكِتَـٰبِ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلْعَزِيزِ ٱلْحَكِيمِ
Subject (Mubtada)
تَنزِيلُ ٱلْكِتَـٰبِ
The revelation of the Book
⟵⟶
Predicate (Khabar)
مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلْعَزِيزِ ٱلْحَكِيمِ
is from Allah, the Almighty, the All-Wise

Notice: There is no verb in this verse. In Arabic, a nominal sentence (a sentence that starts with a noun) makes a timeless, factual statement — this revelation is from Allah, always was, and always will be.

The Cohesive Flow of Verses 1–4

How the opening 4 verses build an unbreakable logical journey from revelation to pure monotheism

1. Divine Authority (39:1) تَنزِيلُ ٱلْكِتَـٰبِ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلْعَزِيزِ ٱلْحَكِيمِ

Establishes the supreme source: The Quran is not human invention — it is a direct revelation from the Almighty and All-Wise.

2. The Human Duty (39:2) فَٱعْبُدِ ٱللَّهَ مُخْلِصًۭا لَّهُ ٱلدِّينَ

Since the Book is sent down with absolute truth, the only valid response is pure, sincere worship dedicated entirely to Allah alone.

3. Refuting False Intermediaries (39:3) أَلَا لِلَّهِ ٱلدِّينُ ٱلْخَالِصُ

Pure devotion belongs to Allah alone. The excuse of taking protectors "to bring us closer to Allah" is exposed as false and ungrateful.

4. Absolute Transcendence & Oneness (39:4) سُبْحَـٰنَهُۥ ۖ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلْوَٰحِدُ ٱلْقَهَّـٰرُ

Allah is far above having children or partners. He is The One (indivisible) and The Supreme (ruling over every created thing).

Core Themes Across Verses 1–4

The profound spiritual and logical journey from divine revelation to pure monotheism

📖
Divine Origin (39:1)
The Quran is not human literature. It is sent down directly by the Almighty and All-Wise Lord of the universe.
⏳
Gradual Guidance (39:1)
The word for "sending down" reflects a caring, staged revelation over 23 years, lovingly transforming people step by step.
🤍
Pure Sincerity (39:2)
Because the Book is absolute truth, worship cannot be casual or divided. Devotion must be 100% sincere and dedicated exclusively to Allah.
🚫
No Middlemen or Proxies (39:3)
The pagan excuse—"we only worship them to get closer to Allah"—is exposed as falsehood. Allah is directly accessible to every seeking heart.
⚖️
Final Justice (39:3)
Differences and manufactured falsehoods will not remain unresolved. Allah will definitively judge between all claims with perfect knowledge.
👑
The One and Supreme (39:4)
Allah is far above having offspring or equals. He is The Indivisible One and The Overpowering Lord who reigns supreme over all creation.
