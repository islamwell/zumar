Surah Az-Zumar · 39:1–4

Ask why each word is there

Go through the first four ayat one at a time. Look at each word, then think about why Allah chose that word, that form, and that order.

  1. 1Read the ayah. Read the Arabic and the meaning.
  2. 2Tap each word. See its meaning, its job in the sentence, its ending, and its root.
  3. 3Answer the questions. Try to answer yourself first. Then open the answer.

The questions follow the way Nouman Ali Khan teaches the Quran: ask why this word, why this form, why this order. The answers are short study notes, not quotes from him.

What you need to know first

Three basic ideas explain most of what you will see.

1. The ending shows the job

The last vowel of a noun changes with its job in the sentence. The colors on each ayah follow this.

EndingNameUsed forExample

2. Most words come from three root letters

The root carries the core meaning. Words from the same root share that meaning.

RootCore meaningWords in these ayat

3. The form adds meaning to the root

The same root in a different shape gives a different shade of meaning. These are the forms and patterns used here.
